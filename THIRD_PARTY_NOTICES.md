# Third-Party Notices

## markdown-it 15.0.3

HTML/PDF direct export uses the pinned, locally bundled markdown-it parser (MIT). It is loaded from the extension package, never from a CDN. Raw HTML in conversation Markdown is disabled. The bundle and its dependency license texts are in `shared/vendor/markdown-it.js` and `shared/vendor/MARKDOWN-LICENSES.txt`.

Source: https://github.com/markdown-it/markdown-it/tree/15.0.3

Reproduce with `npm ci --ignore-scripts` and `node scripts/vendor-markdown.cjs`. Dependency versions and integrity hashes are recorded in `package-lock.json`. The npm browser bundle and source map are copied byte-for-byte from `markdown-it/dist/browser/markdown-it.umd.min.js` and its `.map`. The local source map includes readable upstream sources. License notices are kept separately in `MARKDOWN-LICENSES.txt`. No first-party JavaScript is bundled or minified.
